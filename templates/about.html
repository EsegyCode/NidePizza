Italian Stone Hearth Meets Japanese Sushi Craft

NidePizza was born from a simple frustration: why should one person settle when half the table craves wood-fired Neapolitan pizza and the other half wants fresh salmon sashimi rolls?

From our flagship kitchen at Keizersgracht 412 in Amsterdam, our pizzaiolos ferment dough for 48 hours and bake at 450°C, while our sushi chefs fillet Norwegian salmon and season Koshihikari rice to order.
