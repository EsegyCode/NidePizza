Allergens & Dual-Zone Delivery

Allergen Information

Our kitchens handle wheat gluten, dairy (mozzarella, cream cheese, cheddar, parmesan), fish (salmon, tuna, eel), crustaceans (tiger shrimp), soy (unagi & soy sauce), sesame seeds, and egg.

If you have a severe food allergy, please call {{ business_phone }} before placing your order so our kitchen can advise you.

Dual-Zone Thermal Delivery

Hot wood-fired pizzas are packed in breathable stone-hearth boxes inside a 70°C+ insulated compartment, while fresh sushi rolls are kept in a separate chilled compartment so raw fish and crisp nori arrive in peak condition.
